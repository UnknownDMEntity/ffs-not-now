FFS, Not Now!

A small Windows utility that reminds you to look away, move occasionally and remain biologically attached to your chair in a sensible way.

Release status Privacy Feedback
Downloads paused: We are completing release checks and feedback setup. The website and help page remain available.

Privacy first

No telemetry, accounts, screenshots, keystrokes, app-name inspection or meeting detection.

Smart timing

Natural breaks reset timers, proper breaks are tracked separately, and missed reminders do not become break debt.

Your level of annoying

Toast, reminder window, snooze, pause, fullscreen handling, themes and selectable personality.

What FFS actually watches

Very little, deliberately. Idle detection asks Windows how long it has been since normal keyboard/mouse input. Fullscreen awareness compares active-window size with the display. It does not identify the app.

Installation

Downloads are paused. Installation instructions and the verified checksum will be available with the next published release. Check release notes for its current status.

The app is portable. Windows installer and Microsoft Store distribution are planned separately.
