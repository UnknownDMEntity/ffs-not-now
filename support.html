A little help, without the technical hurdles

Help us improve FFS.

Something went wrong? Have an idea? Write it here and review your message. You do not need a GitHub account to use this page.

Our feedback inbox is ready. Review your message, then open an email draft to ffsnotnowhelpdesk@outlook.com. You send it yourself from your email app.

What would you like to tell us?

Nothing is sent automatically. Fields marked “required” help us understand your message.

Describe it in your own words. Technical language is not needed.
Add diagnostic information (optional)
You can paste FFS → Settings → About → Copy diagnostic information. Review it first and remove anything you do not want to share.

Do not include passwords, personal documents or confidential workplace information. Your draft stays on this page; it is not saved automatically.

Three simple steps

  1. Write. Tell us what happened or what you would like.
  2. Review. Check your message and remove anything private.
  3. Send when ready. Open an email draft, check it and send it from your email app.

No message has been delivered just because you filled in this page.

Prefer to write an email?

Send your feedback to ffsnotnowhelpdesk@outlook.com.

If no email app opens, copy your reviewed message, open your usual email website or app, and paste it into a new email to this address.

Need help finding FFS?

Look for the FFS icon near the Windows clock. It may be inside the little arrow menu. Right-click it to open Settings or exit the app.
