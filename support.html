Support & feedback

FFS does not send diagnostics automatically. Feedback is something you choose to submit.

Report a bug Request a feature

A GitHub account is needed to submit feedback. Reports, comments and attachments are public. Please remove personal details, workplace information and anything else you do not want to share before submitting.
